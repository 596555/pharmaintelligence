# Drug Development, Pharmaceutical Manufacturing & R&D

This track adds a **pharmaceutical product lifecycle** specialty to PHARMAINTELLIGENCE 365. It is designed for a pharmacist who wants to understand how medicines are discovered, developed, formulated, manufactured, tested, registered and monitored—and how data science and AI can support those activities responsibly.

## The three connected pillars

1. **Pharmaceutical manufacturing and industrial pharmacy**
   - Dosage-form manufacturing, process flow and unit operations.
   - GMP, documentation, deviations, CAPA, change control and data integrity.
   - Equipment, facilities, utilities, environmental controls, packaging and storage.
   - Process validation, cleaning validation, technology transfer and lifecycle monitoring.

2. **Pharmaceutical development and R&D**
   - Target Product Profile (TPP), formulation and analytical development.
   - Quality by Design (QbD), QTPP, CQAs, CMAs, CPPs, risk assessment and control strategy.
   - Preformulation, excipient compatibility, dissolution, stability and CMC documentation.
   - Preclinical development, clinical-development literacy and regulatory evidence.

3. **Drug discovery and computational pharmaceutical research**
   - Target identification/validation, screening, hit-to-lead and lead optimization.
   - Medicinal chemistry concepts, structure–activity relationships (SAR), ADME/DMPK and toxicology.
   - Public chemical/bioactivity resources, cheminformatics and molecular descriptors.
   - Responsible use of docking, QSAR and ML as hypothesis-generating research tools.

## Learning sequence

| Stage | Focus | Evidence to publish |
|---|---|---|
| A | Manufacturing science and GMP | Dosage-form process map and GMP glossary |

## Practical product

[Drug Development & Manufacturing Intelligence Workbench](../09-PRODUCTS/Drug-Development-Workbench/README.md) is an educational, standard-library Python MVP. It validates synthetic development-batch records, creates descriptive summaries and ranks documented QbD risk-register entries for human review. It does **not** validate a manufacturing process, set specifications, release a batch, or approve a candidate molecule.

## Scope and safety

This is a learning and portfolio track, not authorization to manufacture, test, release or register medicinal products. GMP work requires applicable local law, approved procedures, qualified facilities, trained personnel and the pharmaceutical quality system. Never invent acceptance criteria or use synthetic training data as a real product specification. Computational predictions, docking scores and public bioactivity records do not establish efficacy, safety or clinical suitability. Clinical and regulatory pathways differ by jurisdiction and product type.
