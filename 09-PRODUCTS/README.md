# Product Portfolio

The portfolio is pharmacy-first and expands across medicine data quality, stock/expiry/consumption analytics, medication safety, workflow design, pharmaceutical development and evidence-grounded knowledge.

1. [Pharma Stock Intelligence](Pharma-Stock-Intelligence/README.md) — stock, consumption, ABC/FSN, FEFO and replenishment review. **MVP available.**
2. [Medicine Master Data Validator](Medicine-Data-Cleaner/README.md) — structural medicine catalogue checks. **Working utility with tests.**
3. [Drug Safety Signal Explorer](Drug-Safety-Explorer/README.md) — responsible public spontaneous-report exploration. **Planned.**
4. [Pharmacy Workflow Automator](Pharmacy-Workflow/README.md) — human-approved administrative process automation. **Planned.**
5. [Pharma Knowledge Copilot](Pharma-Knowledge-Copilot/README.md) — cited retrieval over curated official sources. **Planned.**
6. [Drug Development & Manufacturing Intelligence Workbench](Drug-Development-Workbench/README.md) — synthetic development-batch summaries and QbD risk-register validation. **Educational MVP 0.1.**

Excel is one analysis tool. Python, SQL, Power BI, statistics, APIs, ML, LLM/RAG, testing, pharmaceutical science and domain knowledge are essential parallel skills.

## Product status rule

A product is called an MVP only when code, sample data/documentation and runnable checks exist. Planned products are not represented as completed applications. Every public demo must use synthetic or explicitly approved public data and clearly state its intended use and limitations.
