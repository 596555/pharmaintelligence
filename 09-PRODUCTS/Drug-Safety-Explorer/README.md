# Drug Safety Signal Explorer

**Problem:** Learn responsible exploration of public adverse-event report data.

**MVP:** Load a small public dataset/API extract, document fields and missingness, summarize report counts and visualize patterns with explicit caveats.

**Stack:** Python, pandas, matplotlib and Jupyter.

Include source/date, deduplication approach and limitations. Spontaneous reports cannot establish causality or incidence. Never label medicines safe/unsafe from raw counts or present exploratory signals as clinical conclusions.
