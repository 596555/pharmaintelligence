# Foundations Learning Plan

## Competencies
Data types, identifiers, missing values, data quality, descriptive statistics, distributions, uncertainty, percentages, rates, denominators, chart selection, research literacy, correlation vs causation and sampling bias.

## Free resources
- Khan Academy Statistics: https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability
- Kaggle Learn: https://www.kaggle.com/learn
- OpenIntro Statistics: https://www.openintro.org/book/os/

## Practical tasks
1. Create a synthetic 100-row stock movement dataset.
2. Identify duplicates, missing values and inconsistent units.
3. Calculate stock on hand, days of cover and expiry exposure.
4. Write five findings and distinguish observations from recommendations.
