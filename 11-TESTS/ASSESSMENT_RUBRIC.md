# Portfolio Assessment Rubric (100 points)

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Problem framing and domain relevance | 10 |
| Data quality and provenance | 15 |
| Technical correctness | 20 |
| Testing and robustness | 15 |
| Communication and visualization | 10 |
| Safety, privacy and governance | 20 |
| Documentation and reproducibility | 10 |

**Pass target:** 75/100, with no critical medicine-data error and no privacy/security violation. Any privacy breach or unsafe clinical claim is an automatic fail.
