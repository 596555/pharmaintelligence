# Pharmacy domain quality checklist

- [ ] Ingredient, strength, dosage form and route remain distinct.
- [ ] Pack-to-unit conversion is documented and validated.
- [ ] Batch and expiry remain traceable; no invalid lot merges.
- [ ] Stock, receipts, issues, consumption and demand are not conflated.
- [ ] FEFO flags are review prompts and respect local SOP.
- [ ] ABC is not presented as clinical criticality; VEN requires expert governance.
- [ ] Forecasting uses time-based validation and compares a baseline.
- [ ] FAERS reports are not treated as incidence or proof of causality.
- [ ] LLM outputs cite approved sources and abstain when unsupported.
- [ ] No patient-identifiable or confidential data is present.
