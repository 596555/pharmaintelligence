# Pharmaceutical Data

Study medicine master data (ingredient, brand, strength, dosage form, route, pack), batch data (lot, expiry, quantity, supplier, location), pharmacovigilance concepts, controlled vocabularies, units, provenance and data dictionaries.

## Public sources
- openFDA API: https://open.fda.gov/apis/
- FDA FAERS: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/questions-and-answers-fdas-adverse-event-reporting-system-faers
- WHO: https://www.who.int/

## Interpretation limits
Spontaneous reports are affected by under-reporting, duplicates, missing information and confounding. Counts are not incidence rates and cannot establish causality. Never use exploratory signals to make treatment decisions.
