# 365-Day Roadmap — Pharmacist to AI-enabled Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Development Specialist

**Capacity:** 10 hours/week, approximately 520 hours/year. **Budget:** zero. **Approach:** learn a skill, apply it to a pharmacy/pharmaceutical question, test the output, document it and publish a portfolio artifact.

The core professional identity remains pharmacy informatics and pharmaceutical data analytics. Manufacturing/R&D is an additional domain specialization, not a reason to skip the foundations in data, programming, statistics and pharmaceutical sciences.

## Dedicated specialization block

Complete the [12-week Pharmaceutical Manufacturing, R&D and Drug Discovery plan](../12-DRUG-DEVELOPMENT-RD/LEARNING_PLAN_12_WEEKS.md) as an integrated block of roughly 120 hours. It covers:
- Pharmaceutical manufacturing, dosage forms, unit operations and GMP.
- Pharmaceutical development, QbD, analytical development, stability and CMC.
- Target identification, screening, hit-to-lead, lead optimization and SAR.
- ADME/DMPK, preclinical and clinical-development literacy.
- Public-data cheminformatics, molecular descriptors and careful ML evaluation.

## Weekly 10-hour allocation

- 2 h: structured learning from free sources.
- 5 h: implementation against a pharmacy/pharmaceutical dataset or problem.
- 1 h: testing and error analysis.
- 1 h: pharmaceutical interpretation and limitations.
- 1 h: documentation, GitHub and communication.

## Role identity

Target a credible entry profile such as **Pharmacy Data Analyst / Pharmacy Informatics Associate / Healthcare Data Analyst**, then build toward **Pharmaceutical Development Data Analyst / Manufacturing Data Analyst / R&D Informatics Associate** through demonstrated projects and relevant supervised experience. Do not claim to be a GMP specialist, drug-discovery scientist or clinical AI expert after introductory training.

## Graduation rule

A skill is complete only when you can explain its assumptions, reproduce its result, test edge cases, describe clinical/quality/operational limitations and show the artifact to another person. Manufacturing, product release and clinical development require qualified teams, approved procedures and applicable regulatory oversight; this repository is a learning portfolio, not operational authorization.
