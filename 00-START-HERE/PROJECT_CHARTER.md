# Project Charter

## Vision
Build a demonstrable portfolio at the intersection of pharmacy, healthcare data, AI and digital operations.

## Target positioning after 12 months
**Junior Pharmaceutical & Healthcare Data Analyst** or **Digital Pharmacy Solutions Associate**. This is a realistic development target, not a claim of expert-level AI or clinical software engineering.

## Starting constraints
- General pharmacy graduate with community-pharmacy training.
- Beginner-to-intermediate digital skills; intermediate English.
- 10 hours weekly, approximately 520 hours annually.
- Zero budget: prioritize free tools.
- Environment: Windows 11, 64-bit.

## Strategic tracks
1. **Primary — Data analytics:** Excel, Power Query, Power BI, SQL, Python, statistics and healthcare datasets.
2. **Secondary — Digital operations:** process mapping, data quality, workflow automation and operational dashboards.
3. **Long-term — Advanced domains:** pharmacovigilance analytics first; bioinformatics/drug discovery after core programming, statistics and biology foundations.

## Five products
1. Pharma Stock Intelligence — inventory and expiry analytics using synthetic data.
2. Drug Safety Signal Explorer — exploratory analysis of public adverse-event reports, with strict interpretation limits.
3. Pharmacy Workflow Automator — low-risk administrative workflow prototype.
4. Medicine Master Data Cleaner — validation and standardization of medicine catalogue records.
5. Pharma Knowledge Copilot — retrieval assistant over approved public references, with citations and abstention.

## Definition of done
Every product includes a problem statement, intended user, data provenance/dictionary, reproducible code, tests, limitations, safety/privacy notes, demo instructions and a concise case study.

## Non-goals for year one
No diagnosis, prescribing, individualized treatment or replacement of a pharmacist. No production system handling real patient records. No causality claims from spontaneous reports. Do not prioritize advanced drug discovery over employable analytics fundamentals.
