# Project Charter

## Outcome after 12 months
Target role: **Junior Pharmaceutical & Healthcare Data Analyst** or **Digital Pharmacy Solutions Associate**. This is a realistic development target, not a claim of AI-expert status.

## Starting constraints
- General pharmacy graduate with community-pharmacy training.
- 10 hours weekly (about 520 hours/year).
- Zero budget; prioritize free tools.
- Windows 11, 64-bit.
- Beginner-to-intermediate digital skills; intermediate English.

## Strategic tracks
1. **Primary — Analytics:** Excel, Power Query, Power BI, SQL, Python, statistics and healthcare datasets.
2. **Secondary — Digital operations:** process mapping, data quality, workflow automation and dashboards.
3. **Later extension:** pharmacovigilance analytics, then bioinformatics/drug-discovery data after foundations.

## Five portfolio products
1. Pharma Stock Intelligence.
2. Drug Safety Signal Explorer.
3. Pharmacy Workflow Automator.
4. Medicine Master Data Cleaner.
5. Pharma Knowledge Copilot.

Every product must include a problem statement, data provenance/dictionary, reproducible code, tests, limitations, safety notes and demo instructions.

## Out of scope in year one
No diagnosis, prescribing, individualized treatment or autonomous dispensing. No real patient data. No causality claims from spontaneous reports. Do not prioritize advanced drug discovery over employable analytics fundamentals.
